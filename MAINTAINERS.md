## Responsibilities

- Triage issues and review pull requests in a timely manner.
- Keep dependencies and GitHub Actions up to date (merge Dependabot PRs after CI passes).
- Respond to security reports within the timeframe promised in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Uphold the Code of Conduct.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors who have landed several quality PRs and shown good judgement in reviews may
be invited by the existing maintainers. Maintainers who are inactive for an extended period may
be moved to emeritus status.
